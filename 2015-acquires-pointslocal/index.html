Transparensee Systems, Inc., Acquires Pointslocal

NEW YORK, NY, January 20, 2015 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Transparensee Systems, Inc., a New York based software company, today announced the acquisition of Pointslocal LLC, based in Tampa, Florida.

Transparensee is focused on search, match, discovery and recommendation for data intensive applications, through enterprise software and turnkey, cloud-based platforms.

Pointslocal offers solutions in events, hyperlocal and topic-based publishing. With automation tools that enable efficient aggregation, authoring, categorization and management, Pointslocal's platform delivers mashups of original, traditional and social content that deepens audience engagement.

"On behalf of our customers and partners, we are delighted to extend the breadth and depth of Transparensee's capabilities to include highly automated data and content collection, natural language processing, taxonomy management, content curation tools, and web page publishing capabilities. It's a natural extension of the Transparensee platform," said Steve Lavine, CEO of Transparensee. "Our team is very excited about working with the Pointlocal team. They're a talented group and a great cultural fit."

"Joining the Transparensee team is a great win for our customers and partners as we gain access to advanced capabilities in search, discovery, and personalization. We have complementary technologies and synergies," said Michael Schlein, Jr., CEO of Pointslocal. "Both teams have already been working together and this acquisition brings us closer, which will benefit all of our customers, employees, and partners."

About Transparensee Systems, Inc.

Transparensee Systems, Inc., delivers high speed search and match software for web and mobile applications. Transparensee's Discovery Search Engine is a patented, "fuzzy" search technology that enables users to intuitively refine their search - even if there are no exact matches. Transparensee's Enclosure Platform leverages the Discovery Search Engine to deliver a turnkey solution for data intensive applications like real estate searches for both desktop and mobile. Located in NYC, Transparensee is privately held and has been delivering high-quality user experiences since 2006. Visit us at www.transparensee.com and www.enclosure.io.

About Pointslocal

Pointslocal provides publishers the platform and tools for collecting, curating, and managing content and data in compelling presentations in hyperlocal, topic-based, social and event based areas. With an emphasis on automation, customers enjoy the benefits of harvesting tools, automatically tagged content, drag and drop design, and custom workflow capabilities that include reverse publishing. Located in Tampa, FL, Pointslocal is privately held and has been providing solutions since 2008. Visit us at www.pointslocal.com.
